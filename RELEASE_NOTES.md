# The World Takes Shape — v2.0.0

A new **19.3-second industrial motion study**, with music from **“Centuries” by Fall Out Boy**.

The sequence moves from cylinder pressure and the separate condenser into sun-and-planet gearing, the flywheel, the line shaft, and a worker engaging a local lathe drive. Animated engineering diagrams and procedural 3D geometry share a continuous, beat-linked timeline.

## Included

- A portable browser project with Chinese playback controls and English film graphics.
- Local Three.js, Archivo font, and the 19.3-second audio excerpt; no npm installation is needed for playback.
- Optional deterministic rendering and browser-check tools.
- Historical sources, third-party notices, and release verification data in the repository.
